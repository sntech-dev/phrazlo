Privacy Policy — BombFieldSweeper

Last updated: October 2026

SNTech ("we", "our", or "us") built the BombFieldSweeper app as a free Android game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

BombFieldSweeper does not require an account and has no servers of its own. We do not operate a backend that receives your gameplay data. The game stores the following on your device only:

  • Game data stored on your device: Your progress, such as daily challenge results, win streaks, lifelines, difficulty selection, and settings, is saved locally on your device. We have no access to it and it is never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app permanently removes this data.

We do not collect any other information from you at this time.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location
  • We do not collect payment information
  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in
  • We do not currently use advertising networks, analytics, or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell your data to third parties

How We Use Information

  • To save your game progress, streaks, and settings on your device
  • To generate the daily challenge board, which is calculated on your device from the current date and does not require any data to be sent or received

Third Party Services

BombFieldSweeper does not currently include any third party services or SDKs that collect data about you. The game is distributed through Google Play, which handles installation and updates under its own privacy policy.

Advertising (Planned Future Change)

The game is currently free of ads. We plan to add advertising in a future update to keep the game free, including optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to earn a retry) and possibly other ad formats. If we do, we will use Google AdMob, and the following will apply from the version in which ads are introduced:

  • Data collected by Google AdMob: AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and to show personalised ads where you have consented. This data is collected directly by Google, which acts as an independent controller. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Consent: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires consent for personalised advertising, you will be shown a consent message before any ads are requested. You will be able to change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the game's settings.
  • Device controls: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you opt out, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

We will update this Privacy Policy, including the date at the top of this page, before ads are released, so this section will describe exactly what applies at that time.

Data Storage and Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device and is protected by Android's standard app sandboxing. We do not store your data on our own servers.

Data Retention

Because we do not collect your data on our servers, we do not retain any personal data about you. Your local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

BombFieldSweeper is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, including when we add features such as advertising. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the game. If you have any questions, please contact us.
